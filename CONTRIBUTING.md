AtChem2 &mdash; CONTRIBUTE
==========================


AtChem2 is under active development and contributions from the user community are always welcome, whether that means reporting or fixing bugs, adding features, improving the documentation, or simply providing feedback.

For instructions on how to contribute to the development of AtChem2, please refer to the user manual (`doc/AtChem2-Manual.pdf`) and the [wiki page](https://github.com/AtChem/AtChem2/wiki/How-to-contribute).
